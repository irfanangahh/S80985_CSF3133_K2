About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university in Malaysia. The main campus is located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu.

UMT is known for its focus on marine and ocean-related fields, but it also offers programmes in computing, business, science and many more.

The university has many students from all over Malaysia and also from other countries.
